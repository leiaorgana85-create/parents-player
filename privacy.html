Parents Player Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Google data accessed

Parents Player requests the Google Drive read-only scope. The app uses this permission only to list and download audio files from the configured Google Drive PLAYLIST folder that the signed-in Google account is already authorized to access.

How Google data is used

Drive file metadata and audio bytes are used only to build the local audio library and support offline playback. Parents Player does not upload, modify, or delete Google Drive files.

Storage

Downloaded audio and library metadata may be stored locally in the browser or installed web app on the user's device. A short-lived Google OAuth access token and authorization state may also be stored locally so the app can reduce unnecessary repeat prompts. Parents Player does not store a Google password, OAuth client secret, or refresh token.

Sharing and sale

Parents Player does not sell Google user data and does not share Google user data with third parties. Data is not used for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Device-local audio and app data remain on the device until removed through the app's storage controls, browser controls, or app reset. Removing local data does not delete the Google Drive master files.

Limited use

Parents Player's use of information received from Google APIs is limited to providing the user-facing audio playback and offline-download functionality described above.
