Parents Player

Parents Player is a private Korean-language audio player for Buddhist scripture and chant recordings stored in a dedicated Google Drive folder.

The app uses Google Drive read-only access to list and download audio files that the signed-in Google account is already permitted to access. Audio can then be stored locally on the device for offline playback.

Parents Player does not upload, edit, delete, sell, or share Google Drive data. Google Drive remains the master source for the audio library.
