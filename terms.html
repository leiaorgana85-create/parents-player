Parents Player Terms of Service

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Parents Player is a private personal-use web app that provides playback and offline storage of audio files made available through the configured Google Drive folder.

Users are responsible for ensuring they have permission to make the configured Drive content accessible to the app and to use the audio files.

The app is provided as-is for personal use. Availability may depend on the user's device, browser, network connection, and Google services.

Parents Player does not claim ownership of files stored in Google Drive and does not modify or delete Drive master files.
