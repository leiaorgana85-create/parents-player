Parents Player Terms of Service

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Parents Player is a private personal-use web app that provides playback and offline storage of audio files the signed-in user is authorized to access in Google Drive.

Users are responsible for ensuring they have permission to access and use the audio files made available through their Google account.

The app is provided as-is for personal use. Availability may depend on the user's device, browser, network connection, Google account permissions, and Google services.

Parents Player does not claim ownership of files stored in Google Drive and does not modify or delete Drive master files.
